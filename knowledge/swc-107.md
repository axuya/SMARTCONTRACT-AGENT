# SWC-107: Reentrancy

## Definition

Reentrancy occurs when a contract makes an external call before completing its internal state updates, allowing the callee to call back into the contract and repeat an operation using stale state.

## Detection clues

- An external call such as `.call`, `.send`, or `.transfer` before a balance or accounting variable is updated.
- A withdrawal function sends Ether before reducing the caller's balance.
- A callback-capable external contract is called while a sensitive operation is still in progress.

## Impact

An attacker may withdraw more funds than allowed, bypass accounting checks, or corrupt contract state. Reentrancy is commonly associated with withdrawal and callback patterns.

## Remediation

Follow the Checks-Effects-Interactions pattern: validate conditions, update internal state, and only then perform external interactions. Consider a well-reviewed reentrancy guard, pull-payment designs, and explicit invariant tests.
